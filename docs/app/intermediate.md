# Intermediate mode

Intermediate mode builds upon Beginner mode, adding more advanced features for users who want greater control over their diabetes management.

It includes all the functionalities of Beginner mode (manual entry of carbohydrates, rapid-acting and basal insulin), and introduces the following enhancements:

![Intermediate mode interface](../img/intermediate.png)

## Insulin-to-Carbohydrate Ratio

You can define your personal insulin-to-carbohydrate ratio (CR). This allows the app to automatically calculate the recommended bolus insulin dose for each meal based on the amount of carbohydrates entered.

![ICR Settings](../img/intermediate_settings.png)

## Meal Bolus Calculation

When entering a meal, the app will suggest the appropriate bolus insulin dose using your ICR, making it easier to manage meals and snacks.

![Meal entry with bolus calculation](../img/intermediate_wizard.png)

## Cortisone Administration

Intermediate mode allows you to log the administration of cortisone, which can affect blood glucose levels. This helps in tracking and adjusting insulin needs accordingly.

![Cortisone entry](../img/chortisol.png)

## Alcoholic Drinks Management

Intermediate mode allows you to define and log alcoholic drinks. This feature helps users track the impact of alcohol on blood glucose and insulin needs, supporting safer diabetes management during social occasions.

![Alcoholic drinks](../img/drinks.png)

All features from Beginner mode, such as correction bolus and basal insulin logging, remain available in Intermediate mode.
